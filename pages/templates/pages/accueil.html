Search for the book in the online catalog, confirm that the status indicates "Available", note its shelf location code, and present your ESMA student ID card at the central circulation desk. The librarian will issue the book to your account in seconds.

Students may borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for a period of 14 days. Faculty members benefit from a 6-book quota for 30 days. Loans can be renewed once online through your account as long as no other user has placed a reservation hold.
