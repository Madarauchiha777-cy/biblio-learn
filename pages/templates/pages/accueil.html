SCHOLARSHIP POLICIES

Academic Inquiries & Circulation Rules

Everything you need to know about borrowing quotas, digital renewals, faculty priority holds, and e-learning masterclass completion.

Circulation Desk Protocol:
Books requested online are reserved at the ESMA Marrakech circulation desk for 48 hours upon validation.

Enrolled ESMA Marrakech students may borrow up to 3 physical books simultaneously for a standard period of 14 calendar days. Loans can be renewed once online, provided the title is not on hold for another member.

Mini-courses are produced by ESMA Marrakech faculty to supplement core curricula. They include self-assessment checkpoints and downloadable reference notes to support research seminars and practical labs.

Access to the staff administration console is strictly restricted to appointed campus librarians, department heads, and system administrators. It provides catalog inventory controls, 1-click availability toggles, and masterclass publishing capabilities.
