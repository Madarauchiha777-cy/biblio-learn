CIRCULATION LIFECYCLE

The Book Loan Circulation Protocol

A rigorous, transparent 4-stage loan circulation lifecycle designed for ESMA Marrakech reading rooms and stacks.

01
Discovery

Query the digital repository (/livres/) to identify the call number, author, and aisle shelf location.

02
Hold Verification

Confirm stacks availability and place a digital hold ticket for checkout validation.

03
Desk Checkout

Present student card at the central desk to complete physical book issuance (14-day loan duration).

04
Renewal Ledger

Extend your active loan once online before the due date, or return the volume to the central stacks.
