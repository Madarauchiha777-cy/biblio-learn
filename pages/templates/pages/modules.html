Standard Operating Procedure

The Book Loan Circulation Lifecycle

A transparent 4-step process designed for students, faculty, and circulation desk staff at ESMA Marrakech.

1
Search & Discovery

Browse the online catalog (/livres/) to identify your title, author, and aisle shelf location code.

2
Stock Verification

Check real-time stock levels and confirm that available copies are currently present in the library stacks.

3
Desk Checkout

Present your student ID card at the central desk to complete the physical book loan (standard 14-day period).

4
Renewal & Return

Renew your book loan once online before the due date, or return the book directly to the checkout desk.
